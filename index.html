15 Minute Beef & Broccoli

Asian, Beef, Lighter Meals · 0 servings

Ingredients
  • Makes 4 portions
  • 1 lb beef flank steak or sirloin .5kg
  • 5 g or 1t soy sauce
  • 5 g or 1t shao-hsing chinese cooking wine sub with dry sherry or white wine
  • 10 g or 2 1/4t sesame oil
  • 2 g or .5t sugar
  • 2 g or 3/4t corn starch
  • 2 g or .5t baking soda
  • 350 g broccolini or broccoli cut into small pieces 12oz
  • ¼ large white onion sliced
  • 10 g 3-5 cloves garlic grated
  • 10 g or 1-1.5Tbsp ginger grated
  • +7 more
Instructions
  1. Cut steak with the grain into 3 2.5”/6-7cm wide strips. Turn each strip 90 degrees and slice thinly on a bias. Pieces should be approx 1”x ⅛”x2” (2.5x.3x5cm).
  2. Place sliced meat in a colander and rinse well with water.
  3. Place rinsed and drained meat in a bowl and add soy sauce, cooking wine, sesame oil, sugar, corn starch, and baking soda. Mix well to coat.
  4. NOTE: I use a seasoned 12” carbon steel wok which is recommended for the high heat of this recipe, but if you don’t have one, use a large cast iron or 12-14” nonstick at a lower temp (450F/230C).
  5. Place pan over high heat to preheat to at least 550F/285C (mine was over 600F). Add a squeeze of oil around the outside of the wok. Add half of the marinated beef. Stir and toss to coat with the oil, moving it around/tossing often. Do this for about 60-90 sec or until browned and cooked about 80-90% through.
  6. Transfer beef to a bowl, add another squeeze of oil followed by second half of raw/marinated beef to wok. Cook, stirring and tossing frequently for 60-90 sec.
  7. Transfer to cooked beef bowl. Return wok to high heat. Add splash of water and wet towel with tongs to remove any stuck-on beef gunk from pan. Add squirt of oil followed by half of broccolini and half of onions. Stir and toss to cook for 60-90sec or until charred around the edges and are tender but still snappy.
  8. Transfer to bowl with cooked beef. Return wok to high heat, add squeeze of oil and the rest of the broccolini and onions, cook for 60-90 sec. Add garlic and ginger, moving/stirring constantly. Add previously stirfried beef and veg. Toss to combine for about 15-20 sec.

Comfort Food, Italian, Main Dishes · 2 servings

Ingredients

  • olive oil
  • 30 g garlic sliced, 6-8 cloves
  • 2 g chili flakes
  • 300 g dry white wine
  • 370 g cooked clams canned, 185g/6½ oz per can
  • fresh parsley chopped
  • 115 g butter Salt 1 stick
Instructions
  1. Cook linguine in a large pot of well salted boiling water until al dente, about 7-8 minutes. Reserve some pasta water and drain the linguine.
  2. In a large non-stick pan, heat olive oil and sauté sliced garlic over medium heat for 2 minutes until softened. Add chili flakes and fry for another minute.
  3. Add wine and simmer until reduced by half.
  4. Strain the clam juice from the cans of cooked clams into the pan (reserve the clam meat for later). Simmer for 2 minutes to reduce liquid by half.
  5. Add the cooked linguine to the pan along with reserved clams, chopped parsley, and butter. Toss well to coat the pasta in the sauce.
  6. Serve and top with additional parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.

  • olive oil
  • more cheese + pepper for garnish
Instructions
  1. In a dutch oven or wide bottom sauce pan over medium heat, melt the butter. when butter is fully melted and bubbling slightly, whisk in the flour and cook for another minute or just until there is no scent of raw flour. Be sure not to brown the butter or flour.
  2. Whisk in half & half slowly to ensure no clumps are formed. Bring the sauce to a low simmer. When the sauce has thickened, remove it from the heat and stir in pecorino, parmesan, salt and pepper. Whisk everything together and set aside while you cook your pasta.
  3. Bring a pot of water to a boil and add in a strong pinch of salt. Cook the fettuccine for 12-13 minutes, or until pasta is desired doneness. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water and strain off the noodles.
  4. Stir the pasta and a splash of pasta water into the cheese sauce. When everything is fully incorporated, taste the pasta and adjust seasoning and consistency as needed with more salt, pepper, cheese and pasta water.
